# Atharva ITS - IT Policies for Employees

Last reviewed by the IT team: September 2026

## Remote Access (VPN)

Anyone working from home, a client site or a hotel has to be on the Atharva ITS VPN before
opening internal tools like the HR portal, Jira or the file shares.

1. Install the VPN client from the Self-Service Portal (do not download it from the internet).
2. Log in with your @atharvaits email and approve the MFA prompt.
3. Choose the gateway nearest to you: Pune, Ahmedabad or Bengaluru.

If it won't connect: check that normal internet works, quit and reopen the client, then try a
different gateway. Never turn off antivirus or the firewall to get the VPN working. If it still
fails, raise a ticket and attach a screenshot of the error message.

## Passwords

- Minimum 14 characters. Passphrases are encouraged.
- Passwords expire every 180 days.
- Reset your own password through the Self-Service Portal at any time.
- Five wrong attempts locks the account for 15 minutes.
- If self-service doesn't work, call the service desk on extension 4040.

The service desk will **never** ask for your password or an MFA code. If someone does, it is a
phishing attempt. Report it to security@atharvaits.example.

## MFA

MFA is required for email, VPN, the cloud consoles and anything holding client data. Allowed
methods are the company authenticator app and FIDO2 hardware keys issued by IT. SMS codes are
only for account recovery and must be enabled by the service desk.

## Installing Software

Only install software listed in the Self-Service Portal. For anything else, raise a software
request. It needs your manager's approval and a security review, which usually takes 3 working
days. Local admin rights are not given out just to install something that isn't approved.

## Lost or Stolen Laptop

Tell the service desk and your manager straight away, even outside office hours. Give the asset
tag (the sticker under the laptop) and where you last had it. IT will lock and wipe the device
and inform the security team. Don't try to track or recover it yourself.
